In this second module, you turn letters into an active reading tool. Ukrainian orthography follows a direct, reliable principle: you read by the beats of the word (syllables), and every beat is anchored by a vowel sound. Building systematically upon the seven foundation letters from Module 1 (**А**, **О**, **У**, **М**, **Л**, **С**, **Н**), this module unlocks 16 new letters across five structured lessons: **К, И, Р** (Lesson 1), **Б, В, Д, І** (Lesson 2), **П, Т, Г, Ґ** (Lesson 3), and **Е, З, Ж, Ш, Х** (Lesson 4), culminating in a comprehensive reading synthesis and your first authentic social dialogue (Lesson 5).

By the end of this module, you can:

- Count the **vowels**. That is how many **beats** (склади́ / syllables) the word has (**«У сло́ві сті́льки складів, скі́льки в ньому голосних звуків»**);
- Read Ukrainian by the **beats of the word** (склади́ / syllables) rather than guessing letter-by-letter;
- Hear and say **и** vs **і**: **и** is a relaxed “ih”; **і** is “ee”. Pair: **дим / дім** (also **лис / ліс**, **бик / бік**);
- Keep voiced endings strong: final **б** in **дуб** still sounds like **б**, not **п** (just like **сад** and **дід**);
- Tell **Г** and **Ґ** apart: **Г** is a soft throat sound; **Ґ** is a hard **g** like English *go*. **гора́** vs **ґа́ва** (and **ґрунт**);
- Keep **ж** and **ш** crisp and hard: **ж** and **ш** stay “zh” and “sh”; they do not go soft in these words (**жа́ба**, **шко́ла**, **ка́ша**);
- Keep unstressed **о** pure: **unstressed о stays о** without turning into “ah”;
- Confidently decode your first authentic greetings: **Приві́т!** and **До́брого ра́нку!**;
- Read multi-syllable Ukrainian words (**університе́т**) and Ukrainian cities (**Оде́са**, **Ха́рків**, **Дніпро́**, **Полта́ва**);
- Participate in your first reading dialogue: greeting, introducing yourself (**Мене́ зва́ти...**), asking a name (**А тебе́?**), and saying goodbye (**На все до́бре!**).
