# Голосна́ Е, шипля́чі та пе́рші віта́ння: Бу́кви Е, З, Ж, Ш, Х

Ласка́во про́симо до четве́ртого уро́ку! — Welcome to the fourth lesson! Over the first three lessons, you mastered 18 letters (**А, О, У, М, Л, С, Н, К, И, Р, Б, В, Д, І, П, Т, Г, Ґ**) and learned to decode words smoothly by counting vowels and dividing syllables.

In this lesson, you add five essential letters: the pure front vowel **Е**, the voiced sibilant **З**, the two strictly hard sibilants **Ж** and **Ш**, and the velar fricative **Х**. This expands your reading repertoire to 23 letters — more than two thirds of the Ukrainian alphabet! Most excitingly, every single letter required to read Ukraine's two most cherished everyday greetings — **Приві́т!** and **До́брого ра́нку!** — is now fully unlocked.

By the end of this 60-minute lesson, you will be able to:

- **Вимовля́ти чи́стий голосни́й [е]** — pronounce an authentic, open Ukrainian [е] without iotating or gliding after consonants;
- **Розрізня́ти дзвінки́й свистя́чий [з] та глухи́й задньоязико́вий [х]** — articulate the voiced sibilant [з] and the voiceless velar fricative [х];
- **Дотри́муватися станда́рту тверди́х шипля́чих [ж] та [ш]** — keep Ukrainian sibilants strictly hard before vowels (**жа́ба**, **ка́ша**, **шко́ла**, **ти́ша**);
- **Чита́ти та вжива́ти пе́рші лега́льні віта́ння** — confidently read, pronounce, and understand **Приві́т!** and **До́брого ра́нку!**;
- **Чита́ти нови́й пласт побуто́вої ле́ксики** — decode common Ukrainian words like **хліб**, **ха́та**, **зуб**, **село́**, **мо́ре**, **не́бо**, and **зеле́на**.

## Голосни́й звук [е] та бу́ква Е

Let us welcome our sixth simple vowel: **Е**.

| Бу́ква — Letter | На́зва — Name | Звук — Sound | Характери́стика — Phonetic nature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Е, е** | е | **[е]** | Відкри́тий голосни́й пере́днього ря́ду — Open-mid front vowel |

In Ukrainian phonetics, **[е] is a clean, open vowel**. Unlike Russian or English, where an *e* often introduces an automatic *y*-glide ([je]), Ukrainian consonants before **Е** remain completely hard in standard pronunciation:

- **се-ло́ = село́** (village);
- **не́-бо = не́бо** (sky);
- **ле-ле́-ка = леле́ка** (stork, 3 syllables);
- **ме-тро́ = метро́** (metro / subway);
- **те-а́тр = теа́тр** (theater, 2 syllables);
- **зе-ле́-на = зеле́на** (green, feminine).

Notice how open and relaxed your jaw is when pronouncing **се-ло́** or **не́-бо**.

## Дзвінкі́ та глухі́: Бу́кви З і Х

Now meet two distinct consonants:

| Бу́ква — Letter | На́зва — Name | Звук — Sound | О́пис — Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **З, з** | зе | **[z]** | Дзвінки́й свистя́чий (па́ра до **С**) — Voiced alveolar sibilant |
| **Х, х** | ха | **[x]** | Глухи́й задньоязико́вий щіли́нний — Voiceless velar fricative |

The letter **З** is the buzzing, voiced partner of the voiceless hiss **С** you learned in Module 1:
- **зуб** (tooth, 1 syllable; note that final **б** remains voiced: [зуб], not [зуп]).

The letter **Х** represents a voiceless velar fricative made by raising the back of your tongue toward the soft palate:
- **хліб** (bread, 1 syllable; final **б** remains voiced: [хл′іб]);
- **ха́-та = ха́та** (traditional house / cottage).

## За́вжди тверді́ шипля́чі: Бу́кви Ж і Ш

Ukrainian phonology features an immutable phonetic rule regarding its two primary sibilants:

| Бу́ква — Letter | На́зва — Name | Звук — Sound | Пра́вило — Golden rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ж, ж** | же | **[ʒ]** | Дзвінки́й шипля́чий — **За́вжди тверди́й** |
| **Ш, ш** | ша | **[ʃ]** | Глухи́й шипля́чий — **За́вжди тверди́й** |

:::important
**Шипля́чі в украї́нській мо́ві не пом'я́кшуються!**
In standard Ukrainian literary pronunciation, **Ж** and **Ш** are strictly hard consonants. When they appear before vowels like **а**, **о**, **у**, **е**, or **и**, your tongue tip stays lowered and firm:
- **жа́-ба = жа́ба** (frog);
- **шко́-ла = шко́ла** (school);
- **ка́-ша = ка́ша** (porridge);
- **ти́-ша = ти́ша** (quiet / silence);
- **наш** (our, 1 syllable).

Never pronounce **ка́ша** or **жа́ба** with soft, slushy sibilants. Keep them deep, firm, and resonant!
:::

## Пе́рші лега́льні віта́ння: Приві́т! та До́брого ра́нку!

Now comes the most rewarding milestone of your reading journey! In Module 1, greetings were deliberately withheld so you would never memorize unanalyzed conversational phrases. Today, every single letter of Ukraine's two classic greetings is fully unlocked:

### 1. **Приві́т!** — Hi! / Hello! (informal)
Let us decode it letter by letter:
- **П** — voiceless stop [p]
- **Р** — alveolar trill [r]
- **И** — relaxed front vowel [ɪ] (unstressed, so pronounced smoothly)
- **В** — labial sonorant [v′] (softened by the following **І**)
- **І** — high front vowel [i] (carries the word's acute stress: **При-ві́т**)
- **Т** — crisp voiceless stop [t]

Two syllables: **При-ві́т!**. Use **Приві́т!** when greeting peers, friends, classmates, and family.

### 2. **До́брого ра́нку!** — Good morning! (universal)
Let us decode each word:
- **До́б-ро-го**: 3 syllables (**до́б-ро-го**), stress on the first **о**. Letters: Д, О, Б, Р, О, Г, О. Notice the warm pharyngeal [ɦ] in the final syllable.
- **ра́н-ку**: 2 syllables (**ра́н-ку**), stress on **а**. Letters: Р, А, Н, К, У.

Use **До́брого ра́нку!** in the morning with anyone — teachers, neighbors, colleagues, or strangers on the street.
