## Source evidence and interpretation

The official SUM-20 lookup was run first for 51 relevant headwords. Only акторка returned an exact ingested entry. Dictionary receipts contain paraphrases and provenance rather than reproduced articles.

W03 finds 297/305 lowercase tokens. Its eight misses are names; W02 verifies all ten capitalized names queried. Case-sensitive text-screen misses and uppercase stress notation are not condemned as malformed words. S03 covers the final polysyllabic token inventory. The question repairs use answer-bearing Ukrainian dialogue. Modal contexts contradict each wrong modal meaning; sequence contexts fix clock order and exclude before-connectors.
