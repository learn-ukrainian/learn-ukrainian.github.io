приві́т is the informal greeting for a friend or a peer. Meet it by listening first. Listen to the short recording, repeat the whole greeting, then imagine a friend greeting you. Give the same greeting back aloud before looking again. You are practising a first spoken response, without reading individual letters.

до́брий день is the greeting for the formal situations here. Keep the two words together when you repeat it. You can use this greeting at any time of day. Imagine arriving at a hotel reception desk and greeting the receptionist. A teacher is also a formal addressee. With a boss or cashier, choose this greeting instead of the informal peer greeting. Listen, repeat, and say the matching response aloud.
