Listen to the short greeting excerpt first.

> [Ukrainian Lessons Podcast](https://www.ukrainianlessons.com/episode1/)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| привіт (hi)! | An informal greeting for a friend. |

Imagine meeting a friend at the classroom entrance. This is the informal greeting for that situation. Listen again, then say the whole greeting aloud. Learn it by ear as one chunk; the written model is support, not a letter-reading task. When your friend greets you, give the same greeting back.

Now try a different meeting.

Listen to the short formal greeting excerpt.

> [Ukrainian Lessons Podcast](https://www.ukrainianlessons.com/episode2/)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| добрий (good) день (day)! | A formal greeting for this new situation. |

Imagine arriving at a hotel reception desk. Use this whole formal greeting with the receptionist. It also fits greeting a teacher. Unlike the informal peer greeting, this is the model to choose for a boss or cashier. You can use it at any time of day. Listen, repeat both words together, and respond aloud with the same complete greeting.

Next, look at the printed model.

> до́брий день!
>
> — *Жаркова, «Я досліджую світ», 1 клас, ч. 1, 2024, с. 25*

This greeting appears in a textbook list of polite words. The quotation supplies its printed spelling; it does not show a teacher speaking. Keep the whole shape in view without decoding individual letters.

Return to the classroom entrance.

Replay the informal greeting excerpt before meeting the two peers.

> [Ukrainian Lessons Podcast](https://www.ukrainianlessons.com/episode1/)

Two peers meet here. Follow their short exchange with the paired English support, then take either part aloud. Their greeting remains informal. Compare it with the formal recording and the printed model above. For the next choices, imagine greeting a friend, teacher, receptionist, boss or cashier. Choose a whole greeting for the stated relationship, without reading names letter by letter.
