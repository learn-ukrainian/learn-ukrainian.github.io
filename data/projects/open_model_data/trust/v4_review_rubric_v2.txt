V4 reviewer rubric v2 (source-blind Ukrainian linguistic contract).

PASS requires every condition below; otherwise FAIL. Independently inspect the
actual authored row against the bound atomic target facts, qualified evidence,
context and scope. Surface fluency, a digest, tool presence or model agreement
alone cannot establish correctness or source authority.

1. Language and stratum: use proper Ukrainian and match the frozen task stratum,
register, period and genre. For the first standard_correct or correction row,
learner-facing targets, instructions and explanations must be normative
Ukrainian. Erroneous or foreign material belongs only in explicitly identified
analysis/quotation input, with a correct target separately in answer. General
correction is not restricted to Russian interference. Do not impose modern
2019 norms on every historical, literary, dialect or regional stratum.

2. Actual linguistic correctness: check morphology, syntax, lexicon,
phraseology and stress whenever claimed, in the actual sentence and intended
meaning. Check that the row demonstrates the specific atomic target within
its qualified scope, rather than an unrelated fact. Sanctioned Sources/VESUM
word, form or stress evidence supports only the claim it actually verifies;
a word/morphology pass is not evidence of syntactic government, phraseology,
semantic appropriateness or whole-sentence correctness. Contextual linguistic
judgment remains necessary and independent of the author's assertions.

3. Qualified authority: every claimed rule/correction needs matching
source-qualified target evidence. An occurrence alone is not a normative rule.
A4 extraction hashes and A5 span counts/lengths are structural evidence only,
never linguistic or normative claims. The trusted preparation artifact must
already provide the qualified target facts and evidential scope. If authority,
context or normative status is unknown, conflicting or insufficient, abstain
from asserting the norm; the author emits no row and the reviewer returns FAIL.
Never guess, upgrade attestation to prescription or invent a verifier version.

4. Context and protection: correct demonstrated interference only when both
context and qualified authority support that diagnosis. Protect legitimate
literary, dialect, regional and historical forms, shared/inherited lexemes,
names, deliberate quotation and code-switching. Explicit Russian quotation
written in Ukrainian graphemes must not automatically be called a Ukrainian
error or Surzhyk, or be forcibly normalized. Script does not establish language
identity. Names and quoted foreign text do not require Ukrainian-lemma verifier
coverage as though they were asserted Ukrainian lemmas. Apply the admitted
protections and abstain when context cannot distinguish a protected use.

5. Originality and evidence: the row must be original, well-formed and satisfy
every required field. Do not copy or reconstruct source text or held-out text.
Never expose raw source quotations, source IDs or held-out membership. The
independent review does not replace the existing private A3 originality gate.

Keep the task linguistic and pedagogical. Do not introduce political,
national-superiority or propaganda objectives, or new gold thresholds.
Deterministic mechanism tests do not measure statistical model efficacy.

Reviewer output only: emit exactly one line, V4-REVIEW-VERDICT: PASS or
V4-REVIEW-VERDICT: FAIL. The author follows its own row/refusal output contract.
