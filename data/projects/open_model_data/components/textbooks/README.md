# Component: Ukrainian School Textbooks Alignment

**Governing Issue:** [#8341](https://github.com/learn-ukrainian/learn-ukrainian.github.io/issues/8341)
**Parent Epic:** [#6321](https://github.com/learn-ukrainian/learn-ukrainian.github.io/issues/6321)
**Status:** Active Development

## Purpose
Draws pedagogical and natural register data directly from MOES-approved Ukrainian school textbooks (Grades 1–11):
1. In-context educational explanations and tasks grounded in standard Ukrainian pedagogy.
2. High-register natural prose without synthetic distortion.
3. Clean modern Ukrainian literary standard used as a neutral control and positive reference.
