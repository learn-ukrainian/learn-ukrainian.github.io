# Component: Dialect Defense & Regional Vernacular

**Governing Issue:** [#8141](https://github.com/learn-ukrainian/learn-ukrainian.github.io/issues/8141)
**Parent Epic:** [#6321](https://github.com/learn-ukrainian/learn-ukrainian.github.io/issues/6321)
**Status:** Active Development

## Purpose
Preserves and defends authentic regional vernacular across Ukrainian dialectal zones:
1. Southwestern, Northern, and Southeastern dialect groups.
2. Defends legitimate regional dialect words and morphological forms from being aggressively "standardized" or labeled errors.
3. Strict isolation: negative-control replay buffers are drawn from verified textbook/literary sources, NEVER from deprecated synthetic datasets (`uldr_v1_production`).
