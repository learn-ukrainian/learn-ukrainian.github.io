The sixth blank-header homonym, ULIF 153644, is a **different** child/archaic “bread” sense; `папка` is admitted for the folder sense supported by ULIF 153643. The English gloss and source locator gaps in the JSON remain admission prerequisites, and the `:bad` VESUM compatibility-view gap still prevents direct promotion of `папка` and `накаркати` through the current default path.

- `папка` → **ADMIT** for “folder”: ULIF 153643 has `па́пка` and the synonym text `I. ПА́ПКА ( для паперів , документів ...), ТЕ́КА`. VESUM 261374 has `:bad`, but the checked ULIF sense is attested. The separate ULIF 153644 bread sense is excluded.
- `накаркати` → **ADMIT** as colloquial “to jinx”: ULIF 126667 has `нака́ркати`; its synonym section says `НАКРЯ́КАТИ розм ., НАКА́РКАТИ розм .`. VESUM 218874 has `:bad`.

The sources refer to different senses, and the existing full ledger has noun POS and no reviewed English sense. The reason is unresolved archaic/dialectal sense for this practice row, **not** lack of ULIF evidence.
- `вифлеєм` → **ADMIT** as `Вифлеєм`, `proper noun`, “Bethlehem” (VESUM 50726 `prop:geo`; ULIF 8879 `Вифлеє́м`, “місто в Палестині”). `бандера` → **ADMIT** as the surname `Бандера` only, `proper noun`, “Bandera (surname)” (VESUM 16913 `prop:lname`; ULIF 4104 `Банде́ра`, `прізвище`); the lowercase VESUM 16914 common noun is outside this recommendation.
