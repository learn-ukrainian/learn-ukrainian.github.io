## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Purpose

Per Issue #8160 and the [Imperative Practice Specification](IMPERATIVE-PRACTICE-SPEC.md), this audit certifies the pedagogical, morphological, and orthographic integrity of the compiled Imperative Mood (`наказовий спосіб`) practice deck.

A stratified sample of **200 held-out cards** (40 cards per CEFR level across A1, A2, B1, B2, and C1) was drawn from the compiled shard inventory using generator seed 42. Each card was independently verified against:
1. **Український правопис (2019), § 116 («Дієслово. Наказовий спосіб»)**: Validating 2sg, 1pl, and 2pl suffix conditioning, standard variants (*-імо / -ім*, *-ся / -сь*, *-іть / -іте*), and orthographic constraints (no soft signs on labials/post-alveolars).
2. **VESUM (Великий електронний словник української мови)**: 100% of target forms and grammatical distractors cross-referenced against morphological tags and marker exclusions (rejecting `bad`, `subst`, `dialect`, `arch`, `slang`, `vulg`).
3. **ULIF-Derived Stress Oracle (`data/stress.trie` / `ukrainian_word_stress`)**: Validating target accentuation. 192 of 200 items (96.0%) carry verified accentuation (U+0301) or are monosyllabic; 8 polysyllabic items (1 in B2, 7 in C1) gracefully fall back to bare unaccented display due to oracle `not_found`.
4. **Pedagogical Immersion Contract**: Enforcing English scaffolding on A1/A2 and strict Ukrainian-only immersion on B1+.

## 2. Methodology & Verification Standards

### A. Suffix Conditioning & Multi-Key Acceptance (§116 Правопис 2019)
The audit verifies that:
- **2nd Person Singular (`2sg` / `impr:s:2`)**: Correctly selects `-и` under end stress (*неси́, скажи́*) or sonorant cluster stems (*прові́три*), and zero ending / `-ь` / `-й` elsewhere (*бі́гай, стань, ріж, постав*).
- **1st Person Plural (`1pl` / `impr:p:1`)**: Recognizes synthetic cohortative forms in `-мо` (*бі́гаймо*), `-імо` (*натякні́мо*), and the shorter literary variant **`-ім`** (*натякні́м*).
- **2nd Person Plural (`2pl` / `impr:p:2`)**: Recognizes `-те` (*бі́гайте*) and `-іть` (*натякні́ть*), along with the standard longer literary variant **`-іте`** (*натякні́те*).
- **Reflexive Suffixes**: Both **`-ся`** and **`-сь`** are accepted (*захисті́мося / захисті́мось / захисті́мся*).
- **Substandard Variant Rejection**: Forms marked `subst` in VESUM (such as *ходімте* ❌) are rejected by the generator's marker filter.

### B. Distractor Taxonomy & Zero-Collision Guarantee
Every multiple-choice item provides exactly 4 options (1 correct, 3 distractors). The audit verifies:
1. **Zero Collision**: No distractor text matches any accepted answer (both in raw unicode and normalized unstressed text).
2. **Grammatical Taxonomy**: Each distractor belongs to an authorized misconception class:
   - `WRONG_PERSON`: Authentic imperative of another slot for the same lemma (e.g., target `бі́гай` [2sg] vs distractor `бі́гайте` [2pl] or `бі́гаймо` [1pl]).
   - `WRONG_MOOD`: Indicative mood forms (present or synthetic future) highlighting the indicative/imperative distinction (`бі́гаєш` pres.2sg vs `бі́гай` impr.2sg; `захистимо́сь` futr.1pl vs `захисті́мося` impr.1pl).
   - `ORTHO_SOFT_SIGN`: Erroneous soft signs on labials (*поставь* ❌) or post-alveolars (*ріжь* ❌).
   - `STEM_CLUSTER`: Erroneously dropped `-и` after consonant clusters (*провітр* ❌).
   - `CALQUE_AUX`: Russian auxiliary calques (*давайте робити* ❌).
3. **Clear Explanations**: Distractors carry accurate explanations in Ukrainian (and English glosses on A1/A2).

### 3.1 Curated Lemma Replacements & PULS CEFR Alignment

During population curation of the 1,050-verb fixture (`1000_verb_imperatives.json`), 5 non-pedagogical, vulgar, or passive-reflexive verbs (`матюкатися`, `остогиднути`, `використовуватися`, `виконуватися`, `зберігатися`) were replaced with active communicative verbs:
- `розмовляти`: PULS CEFR A2; placed in B1 practice tier for conversational fluency.
- `сподіватися`: PULS CEFR A2; placed in B1 practice tier for reflexive cohortatives.
- `спілкуватися`: PULS CEFR B1; placed in B1 practice tier.
- `вітатися`: PULS CEFR B1; placed in B1 practice tier.
- `прагнути`: absent from the PULS headword index; placed in C1 tier for elevated/literary register.

*Sample distribution note*: Among the 200 held-out stratified items, only `прагнути:imperative:1pl` (`пра́гнімо`, C1) falls into the sampled set; the remaining four verbs reside in the broader 1,050-lemma population deck.
