# Atlas encyclopedia source-policy verdict

Issue: #4387
Lane: Codex advisor
Date: 2026-08-26

## Verdicts

1. **Easy bucket:** Allow `newsum`, `vts`, `ukreng`, `synonyms`, `phraseology`, `orthography`, `orthoepy`, `holoskevych`, and `davydov` as role-specific slovnyk.me inputs, while forbidding `sum`; the specialized sources must remain specialized cards or notes rather than being promoted to an unqualified definition.
2. **Hero gloss:** Forbid СУМ-11 from leading a page whether СУМ-20 exists or is absent; when СУМ-20 is unavailable, use an eligible modern source or omit the hero gloss rather than promote a Soviet fallback.
3. **VTS dialect-first articles:** Treat a dialect-first VTS article as tainted when СУМ-20 has a fuller modern article and do not lead with it; if СУМ-20 is missing, VTS may lead only when its lead is non-dialectal and independently corroborated.
4. **Wikipedia / Wikidata / e2u / literary corpus:** Treat these as a second easy lane available alongside or after slovnyk.me lookup, assigning them their proper roles—concept/context, linked facts, translation, and attestation—and never treating an encyclopedia summary or corpus occurrence as an unqualified dictionary definition.
5. **Soviet material:** Interpret “do not use Soviet shit” as forbidding learner-facing СУМ-11 entirely, including footnotes and historical cards; any retained internal data is audit-only and must not render.

## Residual risk

slovnyk.me is a mirror rather than the authoritative publication channel, so every surfaced card still needs attribution to the underlying work and official-link/provenance handling; СУМ-20 coverage gaps, VTS dialect/register bias, and noisy or non-normative secondary evidence can still leave a page without a safe hero gloss and require an explicit omission or review queue.

No Ukrainian glosses are invented here.
