# Evidence-derived teaching-adequacy review

Accept content when every plan objective and content point is taught accurately, with sufficient explanation for the intended learner, useful worked examples, and aligned practice with feedback. Require explicit connections between ideas and explanations of unfamiliar terms, steps and reasoning. Reject missing coverage, unsupported claims, repetitive padding, decorative digressions, dry lists replacing teaching, and compression that forces learners to infer untaught connections. Judge length by what the teaching requires; neither reward nor penalize a word count.

Consume source-support and repetition evidence from `scripts/audit/module_size_policy_audit.py` and `scripts/build/module_size_policy.py`. Counts and legacy sizing metadata are information only. An explicit `EVIDENCE_GAP` or `UNSUPPORTED_OBJECTIVE` comment identifies an objective or content point and its missing source support. This routes to research or versioned plan review regardless of length. A legacy `SIZE_POLICY_MISMATCH` marker alone is not evidence of a teaching defect.

- Identify each unsupported objective or content point and the evidence needed to teach it. Route genuine source gaps to research or versioned plan review.
- Preserve factual verification, source fidelity, practice requirements, level-appropriate comprehensibility and A1 English support.
- Inspect repeated framing, generic exposition, uncited interpretation and inflated transitions as possible padding. A repetition finding needs the actual passages and an explanation of the lost teaching value.
- Separate authored explanation from verified primary-source quotations when reporting evidence. Descriptive counts may explain an artifact; they never establish teaching adequacy.
- Missing required dossier evidence is a source gap. Dossier density or a numeric ceiling alone proves neither padding nor sufficient teaching.

Keep exemplar-specific evidence, headings and conclusions tied to that exemplar rather than turning them into track defaults.
