## Boundaries that remain binding

- **Source authorship and provenance:** retain immutable source-unit identities,
  hashes, acquisition evidence, exact span locators, and transformations. A
  human-authored source must be traceable as such. Keep source text separate
  from model annotations, hypotheses, and proposed corrections. Preserve
  literary voice, dialect, historical forms, quotation, contact composition,
  and uncertain language identity; script alone is not identity evidence.
- **Rights by operation:** retain separate evidence for retention, local
  analysis, external transmission, derivation, training, publication, and
  redistribution. Public availability proves none of these permissions.
  Unknown or denied rights block the affected operation; keep its residual
  visible while independently authorized safe work continues.
- **Custody and capacity:** protected source text and derivatives stay on the
  designated remote custody host. Local/public artifacts contain text-free
  receipts and control metadata. Preserve access controls, recovery evidence,
  and measured byte/inode reserves. Do not copy the corpus locally or delete
  evidence to resolve capacity pressure.
- **Held-out separation:** preserve source-family grouping, near-duplicate
  controls, sealed membership, and the temporal/visibility firewall. Builders
  and proposers must not receive held-out text, labels, locators, fingerprints,
  or neighbours. Independent evaluation must not reuse construction derivatives.
- **Independent decisions:** separate source/rights stewardship, custody/split
  stewardship, producers, and reviewers. Keep proposals author-blind and
  label-blind where required; no self-vote or majority-as-truth shortcut.
  Preserve independent Ukrainian semantic review and exact-head cross-family
  review for consequential code/skill changes. Neither replaces the other.
- **Admission and overlays:** preserve stable lineage and append-only evidence.
  Authorship, rights, provenance, split integrity, and semantic evidence remain
  separate gates. A model hypothesis cannot independently admit silver or make
  gold; gold still requires source-qualified human adjudication with cited
  evidence and independent eligibility gates. This guidance grants no release.
