# Exhaust the design space

For a novel interaction or structural decision with no local precedent, compare two or three distinct shapes before committing. Use a short written comparison, or throwaway probes in scratch that are not production code. The prototype playbook is `poteto-mode/playbooks/prototype.md`.

Whole-shape alternatives, not point fixes inside one shape. Record why the losers lost.

Do not build parallel production implementations, and do not run an N-implementation arena. If the chosen shape is new architecture, layout, process, or policy, stop for designated approval before implementing it.
