<TabItem label="Урок — Lesson">

In this second module, you turn letters into an active reading tool. Ukrainian orthography follows a direct, reliable principle: you read by syllables, and every syllable is anchored by a vowel sound. Building systematically upon the seven foundation letters from Module 1 (**А**, **О**, **У**, **М**, **Л**, **С**, **Н**), this module unlocks 16 new letters across five structured lessons: **К, И, Р** (Lesson 1), **Б, В, Д, І** (Lesson 2), **П, Т, Г, Ґ** (Lesson 3), and **Е, З, Ж, Ш, Х** (Lesson 4), culminating in a comprehensive reading synthesis and your first authentic social dialogue (Lesson 5).

By the end of this module, you can:

- Apply the golden syllable rule: count the vowels to count the syllables (**«У сло́ві сті́льки складів, скі́льки в ньому голосних звуків»**);
- Read Ukrainian by syllables (**склади́**) rather than guessing letter-by-letter;
- Master the relaxed front vowel **[и]** and contrast it with high **[і]** (**дим** vs **дім**, **лис** vs **ліс**, **бик** vs **бік**);
- Preserve voiced consonants at the end of words without devoicing (**дуб** [дуб], **сад** [сад], **дід** [дід]);
- Distinguish the pharyngeal fricative **Г [ɦ]** from the velar plosive **Ґ [ɡ]** (**гора́** vs **ґа́ва**, **ґрунт**);
- Keep Ukrainian sibilants **Ж [ʒ]** and **Ш [ʃ]** strictly hard in standard pronunciation (**жа́ба**, **шко́ла**, **ка́ша**);
- Confidently decode your first authentic greetings: **Приві́т!** and **До́брого ра́нку!**;
- Read multi-syllable Ukrainian words (**університе́т**) and Ukrainian cities (**Оде́са**, **Ха́рків**, **Дніпро́**, **Полта́ва**);
- Participate in your first reading dialogue: greeting, introducing yourself (**Мене́ зва́ти...**), asking a name (**А те́бе?**), and saying goodbye (**На все до́бре!**).
