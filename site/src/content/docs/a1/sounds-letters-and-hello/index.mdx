<TabItem label="Урок — Lesson">

**Звуки та перші літери** — Перші 7 літер (А, О, У, М, Л, С, Н), відкриті й закриті склади та перші слова.

This module has **5** lessons: Six Vowel Sounds, Ten Letters; Stress: Наголос; Your First Conversation; First Pronunciation Traps; Textbook Check.

## Уроки — Lessons

- [1. Six Vowel Sounds, Ten Letters](/a1/sounds-letters-and-hello/1/) — 60 min
- [2. Stress: Наголос](/a1/sounds-letters-and-hello/2/) — 60 min
- [3. Your First Conversation](/a1/sounds-letters-and-hello/3/) — 60 min
- [4. First Pronunciation Traps](/a1/sounds-letters-and-hello/4/) — 60 min
- [5. Textbook Check](/a1/sounds-letters-and-hello/5/) — 60 min

By the end, you can:

- say hello, goodbye, and a simple "How are you?";
- answer with **До́бре**, **Чудо́во**, or **Норма́льно**;
- ask and answer a name question;
- explain the difference between a sound and a letter;
- recognize all 33 Ukrainian letters at first-pass level;
- know what **склад** means before the next module asks you to count syllables;
- avoid the first pronunciation traps: blurred **о**, confusing **и** and **і**, adding a sound after **ь**, and turning final voiced consonants into voiceless ones.
