This lesson is your final diagnostic and consolidation station for the A1.1
sub-level. You have traveled across six foundational modules: sounds and
letters, decoding printed words, mastering special signs, discovering mobile
stress, introducing your identity, and speaking about your family.

Here, you evaluate your active mastery. Check each skill calmly and
systematically: if you can read Cyrillic script fluently, exchange basic
greetings, introduce yourself, and share a family detail, your foundation is
solid.

By the end of this checkpoint lesson, you can:

- confirm full control over the 33 letters and 38 sounds of Ukrainian;
- recognize and produce core communicative phrases without relying on English
  word order;
- assess your oral and written readiness for the next sub-level (A1.2: My World);
- deliver a comprehensive graduation speech combining identity, origin, and
  kinship.

## Пі́дсумок

Use this final self-check before you move on.

1. Read the short text aloud without stopping at **ї**, **є**, **и**, **і**,
   **ь**, or the apostrophe.
2. Say how many letters and sounds Ukrainian has: **33 лі́тери, 38 зву́ків**.
3. Greet a peer informally and a teacher formally.
4. Ask one person **Як тебе́ зва́ти?**
5. Say your name with **Мене́ зва́ти...**
6. Say where you are from with **Я з...**
7. Say one role or profession without **є**.
8. Say your age with **Мені́ ... ро́ків**.
9. Add one family sentence with **У мене́ є...**
10. Choose **мій / моя́ / моє́ / мої́** for one family word.
11. Close with **Ду́же приє́мно** and **До поба́чення**.

## Пі́дсумок мо́дуля — Module summary

Congratulations on reaching the conclusion of A1.1! This checkpoint confirms
that you have established an authentic, living foundation in the Ukrainian
language. You are no longer deciphering isolated symbols; you are reading,
listening, and speaking as a learner of modern Ukrainian.

Key achievements across the first contact phase:

- **Зву́ки й абе́тка — Sounds and alphabet:** Full control of all 33 letters,
  pure vowel pronunciation without reduction, and distinguishing the hard [g] of
  **Ґ** from the pharyngeal [ɦ] of **Г**.
- **Спеціа́льні зна́ки — Special signs:** Hearing and writing the softening role
  of the soft sign (**Ь**) in words like **день**, and the separating function of
  the apostrophe (**'**) in words like **сім'я́**.
- **На́голос та інтона́ція — Stress and intonation:** Placing dynamic stress on the
  correct syllables, hearing meaning contrasts like **за́мок** versus **замо́к**,
  and using rising intonation for yes/no questions.
- **Пе́рше знайо́мство — First acquaintance:** Natural formulas for meeting
  others: **Мене́ зва́ти...**, **Зві́дки ти?**, **Я з...**, and **Ду́же приє́мно!**.
- **Іденти́чність без дієсло́ва «бу́ти» — Identity without the verb "to be":**
  Saying **Я студе́нт** and **Вона́ вчи́телька** without forcing an artificial "is/am"
  word.
- **Роди́на та володі́ння — Family and possession:** Naming close relatives
  (**ма́ма**, **та́то**, **брат**, **сестра́**) with **У мене́ є...** and choosing
  matching possessive pronouns (**мій**, **моя́**, **моє́**, **мої́**).

What lies ahead in sub-level A1.2 (Мій світ — My World):
In Module 8 (**Ре́чі ма́ють рі́д — Things Have Gender**), you will expand from
people to the world of objects around you. You will discover that every
Ukrainian noun belongs to the masculine, feminine, or neuter gender, unlocking
the ability to describe things with adjectives, colors, and numbers.
