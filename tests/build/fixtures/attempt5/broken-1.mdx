Two peers meet at the classroom entrance. Their exchange uses the informal greeting. Compare their greeting with the printed model already shown. For a teacher, receptionist or cashier, choose the separate formal model. The social situation guides your choice; the printed quotation supplies spelling, not the register rule. First identify who is being greeted. Then recognise the matching chunk in the model. Keep the peers' dialogue informal when practising their turns.
